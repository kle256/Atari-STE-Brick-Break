Brick Breaker is a single player arcade game inspired by the classic 1976 classic Atari game breakout. The player controls a paddle that moves along the x-axis at the bottom of the screen, while a ball continuously moves around the screen, bouncing off the paddle and walls with the angle inverse of the contact point. When the ball collides with a brick, the brick is destroyed and the ball bounces in the inverse direction of contact as well. The objective of Brick Breaker is to destroy all the bricks through deflecting the ball with the paddle before it falls below the paddle. When the ball travels past the paddle, one of the three lives is lost, and when the lives hit zero, the player loses the game. Destroying all the bricks completes the level, and the player wins the game by completing all levels.
